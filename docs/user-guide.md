
# Anonymous Chadabazi Reporting Platform
## User Guide

### 1. Introduction
This platform helps victims report chadabazi safely and anonymously.

### 2. User Registration
1. Open the application.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Verify your email using OTP.
4. Verify your phone number.

### 3. Login
1. Open the login page.
2. Enter your registered credentials.
3. Access your account.

### 4. Submit a Complaint
1. Select your incident location.
2. Enter the type of chadabazi.
3. Enter the demanded amount.
4. Provide the incident date and description.
5. Upload supporting evidence.
6. Submit your complaint.

### 5. Track Your Complaint
1. Open "Allegations You Have Made".
2. Select your submitted complaint.
3. Check the current status.
4. View available case updates.

### 6. Appeal a Rejected Complaint
Victims can appeal a rejected complaint once.

### 7. Language Support
Users can select Bangla or English.

### 8. Logout
Click the Logout button to leave your account.
